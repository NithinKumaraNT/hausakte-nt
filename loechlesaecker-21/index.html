Minor

Living area is stated per DIN 277 and includes an attic "hobby room"

Minor

Areas are headed "Wohnfläche nach DIN 277" — typically larger than the marketable WoFlV figure — and a 12,89 m² hobby room is added to the 68,82 m² to reach the 81,71 m² that drives the 24/1000 share. The section drawing shows DG heights of just 2,20–2,30 m under the Dachschrägen.

How to make the case at the table

This is a friendly, modest negotiation, not a battle. Be appreciative of the flat, factual about the building, and frame €310,000 as the rational number — then hold it calmly.

Open warmly, then pivot to the building. "We really like the flat — the top floor, the light, the parking. Our questions are about the building behind it." That keeps the tone collaborative while you raise the façade and reserve.

Lead with the area basis — it's the cleanest lever. "The area is quoted per DIN 277 and folds in a 12.89 m² attic hobby room. On the living space the documents actually call Wohnfläche, that's ~€4,650/m² — clearly above the ~€4,000 town average. Show us a WoFlV calculation and we'll happily revisit."

Make the deferred costs concrete, not dramatic. "There's a €220k façade quote for the block with the financing still open, and the Haus 21 reserve is €66k against it. We're not alarmed — we just have to price an unfunded, looming cost into an offer today."

Turn the missing papers into leverage. "We can't price what we can't see. Until we have the Grundbuch, the Teilungserklärung and the draft contract, we have to assume the conservative case. Produce them and we'll re-assess in good faith."

Anchor €310,000 and trade only against proof. Present the §02 bridge as the considered figure. If they push back, move only when a document removes a risk — e.g. a WoFlV calc that confirms more living area, or proof the façade is reserve-funded — not on sentiment.
