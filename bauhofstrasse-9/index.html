The risk register

Twelve findings, ranked by severity. Each lists what the document actually says, why it matters, the source, and how to deploy it at the table. The ones marked Critical are the levers that move the price.

R-01

Documented building damage — and the owners voted not to fix it

Critical

An architect's report (Gutachten Architekt Bachem) documents "Schäden am Gemeinschaftseigentum" — damage to the common property — with a cost estimate. At the 2023 assembly the motion to commission Bachem to tender the repairs was rejected unanimously.

Known structural defects exist, are quantified, and were deliberately deferred. Deferred damage does not disappear — it compounds and eventually returns as a Sonderumlage (special assessment) billed by your ownership share. Critically, the Gutachten itself was not included in the document package — an omission that is itself a warning sign.

Protokoll 2023 · TOP 6Demand the full Bachem report + cost estimate before signing. This is deduction (b).
R-02

Reserve fund is dangerously thin for the building's age

Critical

Total Erhaltungsrücklage at end-2024: €21,372.73 for the whole 4-unit building (your share ≈ €5,536). Annual top-up is only €4,000, and €3,199 was already withdrawn in 2024.

A single façade, retaining-wall or heating job easily exceeds the entire reserve. With damage already on file (R-01), the probability of a special assessment in the first years of ownership is high. The reserve is effectively a buffer that has already been spent down.

HGA 2024 · §6 + VermögensberichtWirtschaftsplan · §3Underfunded reserve = you inherit the liability. Deduction (c).
R-03

Redevelopment-zone note just deleted — a betterment levy may be coming

High

The land register shows the property was inside the Sanierungsgebiet "Untere Stadt". The redevelopment note (Sanierungsvermerk) was only deleted on 08.05.2024.

When a redevelopment area is formally closed, the municipality typically levies an Ausgleichsbetrag — a one-off charge for the land-value uplift, often a four- to five-figure sum. The timing (deletion in May 2024) strongly suggests an assessment is pending or was recently issued. Ask explicitly whether it has been billed and who pays it.

Grundbuch · Abt. II Nr. 3 (gelöscht 08.05.2024)Get written confirmation from the Stadt Ludwigsburg. Deduction (e).
R-04

Retaining wall in the driveway needs sealing — unresolved across two years

High

In 2024 the manager was told to obtain quotes for "Abdichtung der Stützmauer in der Einfahrt" (waterproofing the driveway retaining wall). It reappears as a decision item on the 2025 agenda — still not done. The 2025 minutes are absent from the package, so the outcome is unknown.

A retaining wall requiring waterproofing signals water ingress / soil-pressure issues. To be paid "aus der Rücklage" — depleting the already-thin fund (R-02). Water problems recur if only patched rather than properly remediated.

Protokoll 2024 · TOP 4Tagesordnung 2025 · TOP 3Request Protokoll 2025 + the awarded quote. Deduction (f).
R-05

18-year-old gas boiler with a €2,356 repair already booked

High

Heating is a 2008 Junkers gas condensing boiler (Baubeschreibung §21). In December 2024, €2,356.27 of "Material aus Wartung Heizung" was drawn from the reserve. The building uses gas with no renewables (Energieausweis).

A gas boiler at ~18 years is near end-of-life; the large 2024 repair confirms it. Under the GEG ("Heizungsgesetz"), a forced replacement increasingly triggers a 65%-renewable requirement — a major shared cost — while the CO₂ levy on gas keeps rising each year.

Baubeschreibung · §21HGA 2024 · Kontoauszüge 09.12.2024EnergieausweisPrice in a boiler-replacement reserve. Deduction (d).
R-06

An open legal case sits on the community's books

Medium

The 2024 Vermögensbericht lists "Verbindlichkeiten aus Rechtsfällen" of €1,263.23 — liabilities arising from legal proceedings — plus a separate line for legal-case receivables.

A small figure, but it confirms the community has been (or is) in litigation. Legal disputes can escalate in cost and signal friction among only four owners. You need to know what it concerns and whether it is closed.

HGA 2024 · Vermögensbericht §2 (9500-Abgrenzung)Ask the Verwalter for the nature & status of the Rechtsfall.
R-07

Living area is calculated generously

Medium

The stated 99.11 m² includes the balcony at 50% (4.40 m²) and the ground-floor storage room (3.22 m²) as living space, then applies a rough "−3% plaster" estimate rather than a survey.

Under WoFlV, balconies usually count at 25% and external storage typically isn't living area at all. Adjusted, comparable living space is closer to ~93–95 m². So the true price-per-m² of actual living space is higher than advertised — a clean, factual lever.

Wohnflächenberechnung · Whg. Nr. 3Re-base €/m² on adjusted area. Deduction (a).
R-08

Insurance: only a 2020/21 policy shown, with no flood cover visible

Medium

The only insurance document provided is the 2021 premium invoice (Wohngebäude €698 + Haftpflicht €10). It lists no Elementarschadenversicherung (natural-hazard / Starkregen / flood cover), and there is no proof of current coverage or the insured sum.

Given a known water/retaining-wall problem (R-04), missing natural-hazard cover is a real exposure. A 5-year-old invoice is not evidence the building is adequately insured today.

Versicherungspolice (dated Dec 2020)Demand the current policy + Elementar confirmation + insured value.
R-09

Hausgeld is rising fast and was under-budgeted

Medium

Monthly Hausgeld jumped from €342 to €416 (+21.6%) from July 2025, and the 2024 statement still required a €926.50 back-payment. Each year's plan bakes in a "general 10% increase."

Carrying cost is ~€4,992/year and trending up — and the repeated Nachschüsse show the budget chronically lags real costs. This erodes net yield and signals more increases ahead.

Wirtschaftsplan · §4HGA 2024 · §5Factor higher running costs into your yield math.
R-10

1962 right-of-way servitude on the west side

Low

A Winkeldurchgang (passage right-of-way) on the west side is shared with the owner of Bauhofstraße 11 under a 1962 servitude (Servitutenbuch Bd. I Bl. 213 §108), recorded in Abt. II.

A permanent encumbrance limiting exclusive use of part of the plot. Long-standing and low-impact, but it travels with the title and should be understood.

Grundbuch · Abt. II Nr. 1Confirm scope; usually accepted, note for completeness.
R-11

Existing €155,000 mortgage must be cleared at closing

Low

A €155,000 Grundschuld for Sparda-Bank Baden-Württemberg (2010) is still registered against the unit in Abt. III; the four older charges were all deleted in 2010.

Standard for an owner-financed flat, but it must be discharged so you receive the property lastenfrei. Verify the bank's Löschungsbewilligung is arranged via the notary's escrow before any payment.

Grundbuch · Abt. III Nr. 5Notary to confirm lien release in the purchase contract.
R-12

Energy certificate expires now; data is a decade old

Low

The Energieausweis is a consumption certificate valid only until 29.11.2026, built on 2013–2015 usage (62 kWh/m²·a). Windows are double-glazed (U 1.1), not triple; no controlled ventilation.

A fresh certificate will be required and the figures are stale. The fabric is decent-but-dated 2008 standard — modernization pressure (windows, ventilation, the contemplated PV) sits on the horizon, not the present.

How to make the case at the table

Order matters. Open with the unarguable facts, let the deferred-maintenance reality do the heavy lifting, and frame €330,000 as the rational risk-adjusted number — not a lowball.

Lead with the building, not the flat. Concede the apartment shows well — then pivot: "The building behind it has an architect-documented damage report the owners declined to act on, and a reserve of only €21k to cover it." That single sentence reframes the price.

Quantify the inherited liability. Damage report + retaining wall + 18-year boiler + a €21k reserve = a near-certain Sonderumlage. Your 259/1000 share of even a modest €60–100k works programme is €15–26k. That's not speculation — it's arithmetic on their own documents.

Use the area facts cleanly. "Your 99 m² counts the balcony at half and the cellar storage as living space. On comparable living area this is ~94 m², so €409k is really €4,350/m² — well above a 2008 building with this maintenance backlog."

Make the missing documents work for you. "We can't price what we can't see. Until we have the Bachem report, the 2025 minutes and the current insurance, we have to price the downside." Either they produce them (and you re-assess) or the discount stands.

Anchor and hold. Present €330,000 as the considered, evidence-based figure with the bridge in §02. If they counter, trade only against documents that remove a risk (e.g., proof the Ausgleichsbetrag is paid), not against sentiment.
