02

The hidden costs

In Germany the sticker price is only the beginning. Kaufnebenkosten — transfer tax, notary, land registry and broker — add roughly 10% on top, and banks almost never finance them. This is real cash, gone the day you sign.

ItemRateAmount
Why it stings: these costs add nothing to the property's resale value. On day one you are "underwater" by exactly this amount — which is why the asset/liability crossover in §04 starts in the red.

04

Asset, or liability?

The honest question. Your equity (what the flat is worth minus what you still owe) climbs as you repay principal and the market moves. Against it sits everything you've truly sunk: down payment, closing costs, interest and running costs — principal excluded, because that's forced savings, not a cost. Where green overtakes rust, it's genuinely an asset.

Red flags to investigate

None of these show up in the asking price, yet each is a future Sonderumlage (special levy) waiting to happen. For any Eigentumswohnung, check every one with the seller and in the Eigentümerversammlung protocols before you sign.

Deferred major worksRoof, façade, windows or balconies the WEG has postponed. Your ownership share (MEA) of the eventual bill lands as a special levy — ask what's been voted on versus merely "discussed".
